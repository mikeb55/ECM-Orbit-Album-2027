# V1.0 - EP Tracks

**Revised EP Listings**

---

This version corrects the previous over-duplication problem and gives each EP a clearer identity. Each EP now has a distinct artistic purpose: lyrical chamber (EP 1), groove and motion (EP 2), and exploratory extended language (EP 3).

---

# EP 1 - Chamber Trio

**Concept:** Lyrical chamber jazz / ECM-style statement / core artistic identity.

**Tracks:**

1. Glass Engine
2. Sylva Narrative
3. Myrtle's Prayer

*If we have extra time:* Unreliable Gravity

---

# EP 2 - Groove / Motion

**Concept:** Groove-led trio music / more direct motion / rhythmic identity.

**Tracks:**

1. Unreliable Gravity
2. Glass Engine (Trio Version)
3. Myrtle's Prayer (Trio Version)

*If we have extra time:* Sylva Narrative (Trio Version)

---

# EP 3 - Exploratory

**Concept:** Exploratory modern jazz / expanded form / extended improvisation.

**Tracks:**

1. Eviscerating Angels
2. Sylva Narrative (Extended Version)
3. Glass Engine (Extended Version)

*If we have extra time:* Myrtle's Prayer (Solo)

---

# Core Catalogue Logic

- EP 1 = lyrical chamber identity
- EP 2 = groove and motion
- EP 3 = exploratory and extended language
